Innuora – Snapshot For New Codex Session
What: AI-assisted CBT platform delivering personalized, culturally localized mental-health support (EN/FR/AR).
Core Pillars: Evidence-based CBT tools, empathetic AI chat, structured diagnostics/journeys, mood & progress tracking, expansive content library, secure & inclusive design.

innuora-web Repo Scope
Marketing-facing only. Pages & assets for home/landing, product/demo, join/signup funnel, legal (Terms/Privacy/EULA), and content libraries (articles/guides/insights).
No diagnostics/mock data or app logic required.
Tone & Voice
Compassionate, validating, hopeful.
Clinically accurate but plain-language, trauma-aware, culturally sensitive.
Focus on actionable guidance; avoid stigma & clichés.
SEO & Content Hygiene
Localized metadata (title/description/keywords, OG/Twitter tags) per locale.
Structured headings (H1/H2/H3), keyword in H1 + intro.
L10n parity: same slugs/frontmatter across EN/FR/AR; adapt idioms, respect RTL for Arabic.
Internal links to relevant guides/resources; use FAQ schema where useful.
Page Expectations
Home: Hero value prop, credibility signals, feature highlights, demo teaser, testimonials, CTA.
Join: Plan overview, CTA to signup/onboarding.
Demo: Walkthrough of AI session, dashboard screenshots, engagement stats.
Legal: Plain-language summary + full legal text, localized.
Content Hub: Category hubs + article pages with structured Markdown (intro → evidence → actionable steps → “when to seek help” → disclaimer).
Content Library Conventions
Markdown with frontmatter (title, description, slug, category, contentType, intent, keywords, readingTime, publishedAt, draft).
Articles emphasise practical CBT skills; each locale has full native copy (no placeholders).
Consistent structure: context, therapeutic rationale, step-by-step tools, resources/disclaimer.
Visual/UX Notes
Tailwind palette: calm greens/blues; accessible contrast, dark mode support.
Reusable blocks for badges, CTA banners, testimonial cards, stats.
Imagery inclusive & stress-free.
Copywriting Guidelines
Compassion + empowerment (“Here’s how you can…”).
Active voice, short paragraphs, empathetic microcopy.
Avoid medical advice framing; provide safety disclaimers.
Deliverable Checklist
Localized copy & metadata (EN/FR/AR).
SEO-ready title/description/OG/Twitter tags.
Accessible markup (alt text, semantic headings, ARIA where needed).
Optimized assets/performance (compressed images, minimal unused CSS/JS).
Internal linking to key resources, newsletter signup, CTA flow.
